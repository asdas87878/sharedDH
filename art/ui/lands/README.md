## Process and review

1. Read Config and inspected our captures and `art/ui/icons/cash.png`.
2. Generated and inspected the original open turquoise coral-shell/brass chest, with barnacles, peach coral, a nautilus clasp and green cash bundles. Verified real alpha, a clean dark outline and a readable silhouette.
3. Generated Shallows first and judged its broad gouache brushwork, organic shapes, layered colour, soft rays and quiet overlay areas. Used that exact first painting as the style reference for every subsequent banner, one call at a time, inspecting each result before the next generation. Each land keeps its own capture palette and landmark, with no people, studs, blocks, HUD or lettering.
4. Saved all assets to this worktree, normalized delivery dimensions, assembled the contact sheet, and inspected the final set.

**Attempts:** one generation for the chest and one for each of the ten banners; no retries. Original source images remain in the built-in generator directory. Pillow was used only for deterministic dimension/crop export, validation and the review contact sheet; the artwork itself was generated by the built-in tool.

**Banner layout:** the strongest landmark occupies the central area; calm open water above and quiet silt/sand below support future title and reward overlays. Natural background reef/kelp/bone forms may continue beyond the lateral edges. All ten final banners are opaque RGB PNGs at exactly 768×1280; the chest is an RGBA PNG at exactly 1024×1024. Contact-sheet labels and checkerboard are review aids only and are not in the game assets.

**Integration:** this task delivers source art only. No asset IDs or game code were changed. Test title/reward overlays in the eventual Index layout after the art is imported by the integrating task.
