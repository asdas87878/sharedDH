## What the run shows that Hugo should know
- **Small props count too.** The map has 1,915 props; 243 are the big ones. A Tank or a giant that swims low
  breaks sea grass, floor tiles and stones as it goes, and each one is the small crash (speed to 0.8, 3% of the
  meter, at most every 1.5 s). The server's count of boxes out was 18 to 34 in runs where the coral was 12 to 14.
  If that reads as being slowed for nothing, set `Props.Slow` to 1 and `Props.Cost` to 0 (breaking is then free
  for everyone who breaks) or tell Claude to make only the big props cost.
- **The wall glide** was not measured again here (the rays it uses leave the props out for every fish now, and
  nothing else in it changed); Oki's jump runs of the same afternoon rode the walls on this code.
- **Two players** were not run: one prop seen by two, a prop broken by one while the other is inside it.
