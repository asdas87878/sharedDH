# The hop-off boost, the second hook and the wall ride: scripted playtest on grey-box 15

2026-11-13, a Claude worker on Hugo's PC, finishing the movement work Hugo asked for on 2026-11-12
([riding feel](../design/riding-feel.md), "Hop-off speed and the map edge"). Studio was driven only through
`~/Projects/studio-runner/studio-run.mjs` (never Roblox's Studio MCP), nothing was uploaded. No keyboard or mouse:
**nobody has played this by hand yet.** Every number below is from the final code, on the partner's new map
(grey-box 15), unless a line says otherwise.

## How
The driver in `tools/studio/movement_w1/` (a temporary LocalScript and Script, installed for the run and removed
after; its README has the commands). It rides through the sandbox, steers by turning the view the way a player does
(`RideController.look`), presses the real hop (`Actions.jump`) and reads the server's words. Ten playtests. The
output had no error and no warning from the game's scripts in any of them; the one warning there is, an asset type
mismatch on `rbxassetid://203785492`, was there before this work.

## What was found and changed on the new map
1. **A real stall, and its cause.** 30 degrees into a low terrace wall about 2,750 studs down, the fish was held at
   1 stud a second with nothing in its way: no wall state, the steering's rays clear, the velocity it asked for
   untouched. The rider sits above the fish, and the rider's own body had caught on the lip of the ground on top of
   the wall, which the fish's rays pass under. With the rider's body made solid to nothing while riding, the same
   run kept 93% of its speed and slid 166 studs. **Now:** a character that is riding collides with nothing (the
   server's collision group "Riding", `PropService.riding`, switched by `RideService`'s state); the fish's own
   steering (`Lib/Course`) keeps both in open water, as it was always meant to. Off the fish the body is solid again.
2. **Low steps are climbed.** The new lane's sides step up in terraces, and the ground on a wall's top steps along
   it. Something in the way that ends within 10 studs above the fish, with open water over it, is no longer a wall
   to ride or a corner to stop in: the floor rule takes the fish up over it (`Course`, `isStep`). A hop is as before.
3. **Corner slivers only.** The last worker's draft treated the END of any boundary box as its broad face, which
   could send a fish through a real corner of a thick wall. Now only an end met within 3 studs of the box's corner
   counts as the broad face (the joint between two pieces at a bend); more of an end than that is a real corner.
   Of 2,314 frames at a wall counted in the final runs, 4 met an end.
4. **The server's edge.** It told the lane's own edge from a thing standing in the lane by the distance to the
   lane's surveyed width, 20 studs. The new canyon's walls wave in and out, in places over a hundred studs inside
   that width, so riding into them would have been charged as a crash. The map's own boundary boxes (`Lane/Walls`,
   `Lane/Ground`, `Lane/Barrier`) are now the edge, whatever the distance. No wall cost a crash in any run; the
   "Crashed" words seen were props being broken (speed x0.8), as before.
5. **The swing faces the wall.** Square on, the fish was turned along the wall while it rocked. The body now turns
   along the wall only by as much as it slides along it.
6. **The camera is as it was before this work.** The last worker had added a cap on how fast the view may turn near
   a wall (135 degrees a second) and a second spring on the camera's position. Measured: with no look command in
   the last 0.3 s the view never turned faster than 15 degrees a second through wall contact and a hop at the wall,
   cap or no cap, so the cap only ever slowed the player's own look, and the 168 degrees a second in the earlier
   report was the test turning the view itself. The position spring made the worst change of camera speed in one
   frame larger, not smaller (244 studs a second with it, 11 without, same three runs). Both are taken out.

## The hop
Auto-grab off, so the whole window is flown. Speed is the fish's own as the hop began.

| Case | Speed leaving | Peak (at 0.07 s) | Glide pace | Back at glide pace by | Glide ends | Window |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Pufferfish, angry | 32 | 69 | 34 | 0.7 s | 1.53 s, 67 studs | 2.87 s, 107 studs |
| Pufferfish | 40 | 82 | 34 | 0.7 s | 1.60 s, 75 studs | 2.93 s, 115 studs |
| Anglerfish, angry | 57 | 109 | 63 | 0.7 s | 1.80 s, 133 studs | 3.07 s, 204 studs |
| Anglerfish (in a Predator's burst) | 92 | 166 | 63 | 0.7 s | 2.07 s, 175 studs | 3.40 s, 249 studs |
| The Devourer (bursts on) | 187 | 317 | 128 | 0.7 s | 2.42 s, 369 studs | 3.75 s, 501 studs |

Less speed gives less air and a shorter hop, and the boost is whole for about 0.12 s and gone by 0.7 to 0.75 s.
**The slowest hop still reaches a fish:** six hands-off hops off slow, angry fish (32 to 40 studs a second), six
landings, each 2.66 to 2.73 s after the press (run twice: 6 of 6 both times).

## The second hook
Space pressed every 0.1 s from the grab until a second after the landing, on the final code: **85 mounts, no hop
off the fish landed on, no second landing.** 84 were one pull-in and one landing. In one, the server refused the
first grab before any landing ("Blocked": something solid between the rider and that fish), the hop went on, and
the next press, 0.11 s later, grabbed another fish, which was landed on. That is the right behaviour and not the
bug: the rider was never on the first fish, a refused grab must not end the hop, and a press is the player asking
to grab. Hands off, the pull-in waits `Jump.RehookGuard` (0.45 s) before it takes another fish by itself. (The bug
itself, with the guards off, was reproduced 32 times in 32 on 2026-11-12.) A chain made on purpose is untouched:
a press 0.20 s after a landing is refused, and the hop began 0.302 to 0.316 s after it in eight of eight
(`Jump.Cooldown` 0.30).

## The wall ride
A Barracuda (64 studs a second) from the middle of the first room. The walls wave, so the angle that counts is the
one to the wall itself, which the driver reads from the wall met; "design" is `Config.wallKeep` at those angles
times the slide's share near square on.

| Steered at (to the lane) | Real angle to the wall | Speed before | Kept, 0.3 to 1.2 s | Design | State |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| 15 | 23 | 64 | 92% | 92% | Slide |
| 30 | 34 | 64 | 84% | 84% | Slide |
| 45 | 44 | 64 | 77% | 76% | Slide |
| 45, the other side | 44 | 64 | 76% | 76% | Slide |
| 60 | 57 | 64 | 65% | 65% | Slide |
| 75 | 70 | 64 | 44% | 44% | Swing coming in (the blend) |
| 90 | 82 | 64 | 3% | 0% | Swing |

- **Head-on:** no travel along the wall (2 studs in 7 s). The swing's turning points, studs from where the wall was
  met: +7.9 at 0.60 s, -6.0 at 1.67 s, +1.4, -2.4, -0.4, then within 1.5. It dies away in about five seconds.
- **Steering off:** free of the wall 0.06 to 0.33 s after turning away and back at 90% of the fish's speed within
  0.75 s, from the slide and from the swing alike (15 of 16 runs; the other is the burst run below, whose
  "before" speed was a burst's).
- **The hop at the wall:** accepted in the slide and in the swing. A glide steered 40 degrees into the side went
  along it; one steered square on had no sideways travel and ended in the open ("Free"), inside the lane.
- **Further down** (a Barracuda, and a Hellmaw at 130 to 190, ridden straight for 20 to 110 s first; 2,700 to
  15,800 studs down the lane): the same rule holds at the angle really met. Kept against design:
  83% / 82%, 67% / 74%, 76% / 66%, 83% / 83%, and one run at 55% against 74% (a Predator's burst ended during
  it). Where the wall's wave turned square on to the fish, it swung there, as designed. The lane's end is an
  unseen barrier met square on: a swing.
- **Bounds:** never behind a wall or the barrier in any frame of any run. Against the lane's surveyed width the
  fish was inside in every run but two deep ones (7 and 28 studs past it, still in front of the map's own walls,
  which stand outside the surveyed width there).
- **The camera at a wall:** not inside anything solid in any frame. With no look command in the last 0.3 s it
  never turned faster than 15 degrees a second in the wall runs.

## The camera through a landing
The biggest change of the camera's speed between two frames, per mount (12 mounts): median 83 studs a second,
worst 138. With the landing's shake and jolt switched off (`Jump.LandShake` 0, `Jump.LandKick` 0; 8 mounts): median 55,
worst 85. A longer follow time did not lower it (`Camera.FocusTime` 0.2: median 88; 0.3: median 94, both with the
shake on). So what is left is the landing's own shake, which Hugo asked for, and the pull-in arriving at up to
390 studs a second: nothing was changed. If the landing feels too hard by hand, `Jump.LandShake` and
`Jump.LandKick` are the numbers.

## Still as they were
- A and D: D held for a second turned 127 and 129 degrees (the control pass measured 129).
- The landing's slow-down: least speed 0.15 to 0.45 s after a landing x0.79 (`Ride.StartleSlow` 0.80), full speed
  again 1.6 s after.
- Angry mode: a Cuttlefish 47 calm, 32 angry (x0.68), the `Angry` attribute set.
- The archetypes ran throughout (the Predator's bursts and the Breaker's props show in the runs above); they were
  not measured one by one here.

## Not done, and to know
- **Not played by hand.** How the slide, the swing and the boost feel is Hugo's to judge. Every number is in
  `Config.Jump` and `Config.Wall` and in the N panel.
- The swing after a fast hit is less even than after a slow one (a Hellmaw at 172: +18.4, -10.0, +8.7, -1.0, +4.2,
  +1.5): the floor and the descent get in its way. It still dies away.
- A rider's body can pass through the lip of a wall's top while the fish hugs the wall under it: that is the price
  of point 1. The fish itself stays in open water.
- The lane's surveyed width (the soft edge behind the walls) is inside the walls in places on this map, and a fast
  fish can be up to 28 studs past it before it is eased back. It is still in front of the walls. Not changed.
