# Creatures

## Decided
- **Sea creatures of every kind,** the way Ride a Dino does dinosaurs: modern fish first, then huge prehistoric ones, then our
  own mythical ones (2026-11-09).
- **Scale:** at or above Ride a Dino's (its page says 50+ species).
- **The roster:** variant C of the [roster](roster.md), "a giant in every zone": 80 creatures over 10 zones, the last two zones
  our own myths. The first zones have the biggest Common and Uncommon pools, so the start feels vast.
- **Creatures range one or two zones ahead of home,** never backwards, so each zone mixes its own creatures with some from the
  zones before it. Commons only reach the start of the second zone.
- **Rare spawns fit their zone:** an early zone's Epic or Legendary is one of its own fitting creatures (Archelon, the huge
  turtle, in the Shallows), never one from further on. The pool of Legendaries grows zone by zone; Megalodon only from the Vents.
- **Strays:** the extremely rare exception, a Legendary or Mythic from the next one or two zones turning up early.
- **Rarity slides with the zones:** each creature has one rarity and lives in one zone, so the zone sets the mix. Commons only in
  the first zones; then mostly Uncommons, Rares, Epics, and by the end Legendaries with a chance of Mythics. Each zone's giant is
  a very rare Legendary early on. A Secret in every zone, announced to the whole server.
- **The starting fish look nice:** small and basic, one striped and one plain.
- **Late, late game:** our own mythical fish, like a demon fish and a half-skeleton fish.
- **No equipment:** creatures differ by kind, rarity, size and mutation.
- **How they're made:** the [creature method](../style/creature-method.md).

## Ideas not agreed
- **Tags on every wild creature** (the reference's pattern): mutation, size, rarity, name, and the stamina it needs.
- **Rarities:** Common, Uncommon, Rare, Epic, Legendary, Mythic, and Secret for rare server-wide spawns (maybe an Eternal above
  Secret later, as the reference has).
- **Each zone's mix:** the draft weights in the [roster](roster.md).
- **Sizes:** six steps from Tiny to Colossal, the same model scaled; the reference pays 0.25x to 10x.
- **Mutations:** colour swaps that pay more (the reference has Silver, Golden, Rainbow, Magma); our own names to pick.
