# The dock

The hub where every player spawns, with each player's base and the stations.

## Decided
- **Your base stores your tamed creatures** and they earn passive income. It's small.
- **Bases are for show:** no stealing, no visiting. Trading maybe later.
- **A stamina training machine:** you get on a toy creature and rock back and forth to gain stamina. Income upgrades it.
- **Auras** boost stamina (2x and so on), bought with income.

## Ideas not agreed
- **The base is an aquarium tank** on the dock, with your creatures swimming in it.
- **The training machine is a coin-operated kiddie ride** (a bouncy seahorse, a toy Megalodon).
- **Stations,** after the reference's set: sell stand, fuse machine (3 into 1), a submarine or elevator that takes you straight to a
  deeper zone (buy once), the Index (collection book), Top Earners board.
- Tank upgrades: more creatures earning at once, "Equip Best" button, offline earnings.

## Open
- How many players per server, and so how many tanks round the dock.
