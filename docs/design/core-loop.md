# Core loop

## Decided
- **The cycle:** train stamina → ride and tame bigger, deeper creatures → they earn income in your base → income buys stamina
  boosts (auras, a better training machine) → more stamina → bigger creatures.
- **Two separate resources:**
  - **Stamina** is a stat. Riding uses it: the higher a creature's tier, the more it takes to stay on.
  - **Income** comes from the creatures in your base, by how cool they are. It buys the boosts.
- **One dive:** start on your own creature → ride down as far as your stamina lets you → jump creature to creature, taming on the
  way → fall with nothing to ride → back to the dock with what you caught → put them in your base.
- **Going down** instead of across: depth is the progress bar.

## Ideas not agreed
- Call stamina **Breath** (holding your breath fits underwater).
- Each dive ends with a "GREAT DIVE! You caught 6" summary, then you carry each creature over your head to your tank (the
  reference does this; it's funny and it shows off).
