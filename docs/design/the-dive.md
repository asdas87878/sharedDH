# The dive

The run: riding, jumping, taming, going down. This is the part that has to feel great. How riding, Oxygen, the dash and the
grapple feel, with numbers: [riding feel](riding-feel.md). Oxygen is the riding resource (it replaces stamina here).

## Decided
- **Start on your own creature:** pick one of your tamed creatures from the base and ride it down. Tamed creatures are steerable.
  With enough Oxygen you ride it a long way before you have to jump.
- **Oxygen drains while you ride** (2026-11-09, was stamina). Higher tiers drain it faster. A ridden fish speeds up from its
  idle speed.
- **Wild, untamed creature:** you steer it like any other (the user, 2026-11-10; it was "you can't steer it"). Stay on long enough and it's tamed (yours). If your Oxygen runs out first, it
  eats you.
- **Tamed creature:** when your Oxygen runs out you fall off. You float up, with a few seconds' grace to reach another fish; then
  you pass out and wake up at the dock (2026-11-09).
- **Dash creature to creature** to keep going (2026-11-09): a corkscrew dash that refills Oxygen.
- **Jumping off opens a grapple shot** (2026-11-09): aim window, brief slow-mo, a reticle on the best fish, rope, reel in.
- **The descent is gradual and the dive long** (2026-11-09), so the depth lasts the whole game.
- **The layout** (the user agreed with the blockout, 2026-11-09): the dock, a pier to the dive board, then a lane going down
  through zones.
- **The 10 zones** (from the [roster](roster.md)'s variant C, 2026-11-09): Shallows, Coral Reef, Kelp Forest, Shipwrecks, Open
  Blue, Twilight, Midnight, Vents, Bone Graveyard, The Underworld. The first version ships the first four.

## Ideas not agreed
- **The lane is a long valley in the seabed, not a straight shaft:** riding is a forward run you steer, so a gentle slope keeps
  steering and the camera readable. A depth gauge on screen, the water getting darker as you go.
- **Sized after the reference's measured run** (blockout v2): 10 zones, about 23,800 studs, 1,600 deep, about 45 seconds a zone
  at riding speed. A 2-degree slope inside each zone and a drop-off at most borders. Details in the [blockout](../blockout.md).
- **The real-ocean depth on the gauge:** Shallows 0 m, Coral Reef 10 m, Kelp Forest 40 m, Shipwrecks 80 m, Open Blue 150 m,
  Twilight 200 m, Midnight 1,000 m, Vents 2,000 m, Bone Graveyard 4,000 m, The Underworld 6,000 m down to the Challenger Deep,
  10,935 m.
- **Each creature has Speed and Oxygen cost:** how far you get = speed × how long your Oxygen lasts on it. A small creature lasts
  but crawls; a Megalodon tears down but burns through your Oxygen. Starting numbers in [riding feel](riding-feel.md).
- **Riding HUD after the reference's pattern** (in our own look): a ring for time left on this creature, a ring for tame progress,
  a marker on the creature you'll jump to, a landing ring, an "IT'S YOURS!" banner, a "caught" list you carry.
- **Live moments:** "The current shifts!" (schools respawn), server-wide messages when a rare creature appears.

## Open
- How the jump aims: proposed in [riding feel](riding-feel.md) (the dash auto-picks a fish ahead; the grapple's reticle takes
  the fish nearest the aim).
- Revives: the reference gives 3 free, then sells them.
