| 2026-11-09 | The project is shared with the user's partner through a private GitHub repo (`asdas87878/sharedDH`), with the Obsidian Git plugin on both PCs. Studio work goes through the repo too, as there's no shared Studio connection | [Working together](Working%20together.md) |
| 2026-11-09 | The head follows the user's two drawings, the concept's construction. The yellow line runs straight across the face's top, rises along the eye faces, then runs level along the body at the top chamfers. Below: a chin and a jaw split by diagonals. Replaces the rectangular face panel and the turned eye of the row below (and, the same day, a front strip with a separate side wall) | [Workout 2](style/02-creature-look.md) |

The face may leave the body kit's structure; never a snout running to a point | [Workout 2](style/02-creature-look.md) |
| 2026-11-09 | The Sergeant Major's proportions follow the concept: depth 0.57 of the length to the tail base, width 0.86 of the depth, chunky by width, a big thick dorsal | [Workout 2](style/02-creature-look.md) |
| 2026-11-09 | Creatures range one or two zones ahead of home (about a third as often one zone on, a tenth two on), never backwards; Commons only into the start of zone 2. | 2026-11-09 | A touch of luck: extremely rare strays from the next one or two zones only, tagged and announced to the server. A stray Legendary about 1 in 10,000 spawns, catchable at the stamina of the zone it strayed into; a stray Mythic about 1 in 100,000 (the user's addition), at twice that. Never a Secret, never the headline Legendaries early | [Roster](design/roster.md) |
| 2026-11-09 | Rare spawns fit their zone and open up as you go: early zones' Epics and Legendaries are their own fitting creatures (a reef shark and Archelon, the huge turtle, in the Shallows), never a creature from further on, so the coolest Legendaries can't come early by luck. Earlier ones can keep turning up later where they fit, so the Legendary pool grows (1 in the Shallows to 5 by the Midnight zone); Megalodon only from the Vents, Mythics from the Open Blue. Roster now 74 | [Roster](design/roster.md) |
| 2026-11-09 | Every zone has a long tail of rarer spawns: an Epic or a Legendary can turn up even in the first zone, just very rarely (about 1 in 170 and 1 in 500 there), and they become common a few zones on (Legendaries pass 10% by zone 5) | [Roster](design/roster.md) |
| 2026-11-09 | Rarity slides with the zones, as in Ride a Dino: each creature has one rarity, each zone its own creatures, so early zones are Commons and Uncommons and later zones mostly Rares, Epics, then Legendaries with a chance of Mythics. Commons only in the first zones. A Secret in every zone. The roster is reworked to 70 to fit; zones 3 to 10's mix is a guess until we see the reference's later zones | [Roster](design/roster.md), [the reference](reference/ride-a-dino.md) |
| 2026-11-09 | Roster: variant C, "a giant in every zone". 76 creatures over 10 zones (Shallows, Coral Reef, Kelp Forest, Shipwrecks, Open Blue, Twilight, Midnight, Vents, Bone Graveyard, The Underworld); each zone's Legendary is a prehistoric giant, Megalodon in Vents; the last two zones are our own myths (Halfbone Megalodon, Hellmaw) | [Roster](design/roster.md) |
| 2026-11-09 | The roster is at or above Ride a Dino's scale (its page says 50+ species). Progression: basic, nice-looking fish first (one striped, one plain), then the cooler fish of the sea, then huge prehistoric ones, then our own mythical ones (demon fish, half-skeleton fish) for the late, late game | [Roster](design/roster.md) |
| 2026-11-09 | Creature method: references give rules and ideas, never proportions; every creature is built from one kit and judged in the game's view; then a shape pass brings in the reference's cooler shapes as kit changes (the user's addition) | [Creature method](style/creature-method.md) |
