# Style workouts

Small, specific tests of how the game looks, one note each, numbered. Each one ends with options for the user to pick from;
the picks go in [Decisions](../Decisions.md).

**Inspiration (decided 2026-11-09):** Ride a Dino's broad style: chunky shapes, bright readable colour, cheap to make. Our own
creatures, palette and world, never its models or layout.

| # | Workout | The question | State |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Going down](01-going-down.md) | How does it look like going down into the ocean, with a big ocean all round that you can't get into? | First test done: two looks (A, B) to pick from |
| 2 | [Creature look](02-creature-look.md) | How do the creatures look? The Sergeant Major built by the [creature method](creature-method.md) from the user's ChatGPT concept, ridden, seen at game distance | Head and mouth rebuilt from the user's drawings; good enough for now |
| 3 | [Creature set](03-creature-set.md) | Do the rules carry to other body shapes? The Yellow Tang, Sea Turtle and Stingray concepts: three body plans (fish, shell, disc) and their swims | Notes written, nothing built: waiting for the user's picks |
| 4 | Zone palettes | A swatch sheet per zone: floor, rock, dressing, creatures, glow, water colour | |
| 5 | The dock and the surface | The lagoon, the ring reef, the sky, the water seen from above | |
| 6 | Kit pieces | Terraces, rocks, coral, kelp as reusable blocky pieces, cheap to place and to render | |
| 7 | Effects | Bubbles, drifting specks, moving light on the floor, light shafts, glow | |
| 8 | Interface | Creature tags, the ride and tame rings, the depth gauge | |
